## Maximize still fills the whole monitor

Work down this list:

1. **Is it running and active?** Right-click the tray icon. The first line says whether
   it's active or paused (double-click the icon to resume).
2. **Is FancyZones on?** fz-maximize does nothing while FancyZones is turned off in
   PowerToys.
3. **Does the monitor have a layout with more than one zone?** Open the FancyZones editor
   (<kbd>Win</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>`</kbd>) and check the layout applied to that monitor
   and virtual desktop.
4. **Is the monitor or app excluded?** Check **Settings → Exclusions**, including **Only
   monitors at least this wide**, and FancyZones' own excluded apps.
5. **Is the app running as administrator?** Windows doesn't let normal apps move those
   windows, so they keep the normal maximize.
6. **Were you holding the full-screen key?** <kbd>Shift</kbd> by default.

To see exactly what fz-maximize decided, run [`fz-maximize --dump`](/fz-maximize/command-line/):
for each monitor it shows the layout it matched, the zones, and whether maximize is
redirected there, and why not if it isn't.

## The window goes into the wrong zone

- The maximize button, title-bar double-click and <kbd>Win</kbd>+<kbd>Up</kbd> pick the zone
  the window **overlaps most**. Move the window mostly into the zone you want first.
- Dragging to the top edge picks the zone **under the cursor**.
- If the zone's *size or position* is off compared with FancyZones' own snapping, see the
  next section.

## "FancyZones agreement" shows mismatches

Whenever FancyZones snaps a window itself (a <kbd>Shift</kbd>-drag, for example),
fz-maximize checks that it computes the same zone, and the tray menu shows the tally. A
mismatch means the two disagree about where a zone is. Usually that follows a PowerToys
update that changed how zones are calculated, or a monitor that Windows briefly reported
differently.

- Shift-drag a few windows into zones and watch whether new snaps match.
- If they keep disagreeing, set **When they disagree** to **Warn, and use normal maximize on
  that monitor** (Settings → FancyZones integration) and
  [open an issue](https://github.com/fz-maximize/fz-maximize/issues) with your PowerToys
  version and the `--dump` output.

## I can't resize a window

It's zone-maximized and locked, like a maximized window. Drag its title bar to restore it,
hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> while resizing, or turn off **Lock zone-maximized windows** in
**Settings → Behavior**.

## Win+Shift+Up no longer stretches a window vertically

fz-maximize uses that shortcut for a full-screen maximize. Untick **Win + Shift + Up** in
**Settings → Behavior** to give it back to Windows.

## There's no animation

The animation only runs when Windows would animate a maximize itself. Check **Settings →
Accessibility → Visual effects → Animation effects** in Windows, and **Animate the window
into its zone** in fz-maximize's **Settings → Appearance**. Dragging to the top edge isn't
animated.

## An app flashes full screen, or misbehaves

Apps that draw their own title bar without telling Windows where the buttons are can't be
caught before Windows maximizes them, so they're corrected right after, with a brief flash.
If an app reacts badly to being moved, add its exe name to **Leave these apps alone**
(Settings → Exclusions).

## "fz-maximize is already running"

Only one copy runs at a time. Look for the tray icon (it may be behind the **^** overflow
arrow), or exit the other copy from its tray menu before starting another, for example a
portable copy and an installed one.

## Reporting a problem

<Steps>
1. Set **Log detail** to `debug` (Settings → General) and reproduce the problem.
2. Collect:
   - the output of `fz-maximize --dump`,
   - `fz-maximize.log` (tray → **Open log folder**),
   - your Windows and PowerToys versions.
3. [Open an issue](https://github.com/fz-maximize/fz-maximize/issues) with what you did, what
   you expected and what happened, and attach the above. Look through the log first: it
   names the apps whose windows were moved, which you may prefer to edit out.
</Steps>
